Privacy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Playing offline

Battles against the computer, the deck builder, Clan Ascent and The March run entirely on your PC. Your saves stay on your PC. The game sends nothing anywhere.

Playing online

When you play online the game creates a player key on your PC. Its public half gives you a player id (16 letters and numbers); the private half never leaves your PC. You choose a player name that other players see. There are no accounts, emails or passwords.

The free meeting point (a small service on Cloudflare) is used to find each other and pass the battle's moves between the two players. It sees and stores:

  • your player id and player name while a battle is open or listed;
  • your internet (IP) address, to stop banned players and limit how many new player ids can come from one address each day (kept for 3 days);
  • reports one player sends about another (reporter id, reported id, reason, room code, a short note, and the reporter's address), kept until they are reviewed;
  • the ban list (banned player ids and addresses).

The meeting point does not read or keep the battle itself. Moves pass straight through it. Players never see each other's internet address when they use room codes.

Direct connect does not use the meeting point at all: the two games talk to each other directly, so the host's address is known to the player who joins (they typed it).

Update check

The online page reads this website's version.json to tell you when a new version is out. That request contains nothing about you, apart from what any web request shows (your address).

Children

There is no chat. The only text other players see is your player name, and names with slurs or rude words are refused.

Removing your data

Delete the file online_profile.json in your save folder (%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Cards _ Clans\Cards _ Clans) to make a new player key. To ask for a report or ban about you to be removed, contact the game's maker through the project page (github.com/MasterMythic/cardsandclans).
